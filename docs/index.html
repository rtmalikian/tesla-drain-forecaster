Tonight's forecast

Method: baseline = median loss of quiet nights (no factor on) over the last 14 nights. Each factor delta = median loss of nights with the factor minus median loss of nights without it (both groups need 2+ nights, otherwise a transparent default: Sentry +1.5, cold +1.0, Cabin Overheat Protection +0.5, software update +1.0, plugged in −0.3). Forecast = baseline + tonight's deltas, clamped to 0–15%.

What the real app adds

The full app (github.com/rtmalikian/tesla-drain-forecaster) records real nights from your Tesla (or ones you type in), keeps them on your own computer, and exposes an OpenAI-compatible forecast API (/v1/chat/completions, /v1/models, /v1/embeddings) so any AI client can answer "predict tonight's loss" or "why did I lose 6% Tuesday?" from your own numbers. It never wakes or commands a car and sends your data only to the AI backend you configure — or nowhere at all, with the built-in rules engine.
